Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Jewellery & Cosmetics E-commerce Website
Prepared based on the provided requirements and inspired by leading platforms such as Tanishq, CaratLane, GIVA, Nykaa, Sephora, and Huda Beauty.


1. Project Overview
Build a premium, mobile-first e-commerce platform for Jewellery and Cosmetics with secure shopping, elegant branding, and high conversion UX.
2. Business Goals
Sell jewellery, cosmetics, skincare and haircare online.
Provide seamless browsing, search, wishlist, cart and checkout.
Support promotions, coupons and analytics.
Enable complete product, inventory and order management from the admin panel.
3. Target Audience
Women, men, gift shoppers, premium buyers, beauty enthusiasts, and returning customers.
4. Tech Stack
Frontend: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Shadcn UI
Backend: Node.js, Express.js
Database: MongoDB
State: Redux Toolkit
Payments: Razorpay/Stripe (UPI, Cards, Net Banking, COD)
Storage: Cloudinary/S3
SEO: Next.js Metadata & Schema.org
